Investment Approach

Tools of our strategy

15–25 actively selected holdings. Three complementary pillars.

Active selection, beyond ordinary ETFs

  • We select and manage 15–25 companies across EM, SMID caps and equity stories. Diversification must add value, not just more holdings.
  • This combination cannot be replicated with ordinary ETFs. We look for opportunities beyond expensive developed markets, including emerging markets that retail investors often avoid.

No leverage

  • We do not use leverage: we do not want to increase portfolio volatility or potential drawdowns.

No currency hedging

  • We address currency risk through diversification across currencies. During periods of high volatility, we monitor exchange-rate risk.

No shorts

  • Short positions carry the risk of unlimited loss, margin calls and stock-borrow costs.
  • The long-term rise of markets works against short positions. We see our advantage in selecting winners and spend our time there.

Risk framework

Risk Management

Position limits, diversification and independent thesis reviews.

With a still modest amount of capital under management and Patria being the only provider with which we could open an investment account as a registered association, we face high fees. We have therefore had to relax some of our original limits.

Position Sizing

Original: 5% at cost, trim above 10%; initial entry up to 2.5%. Proposal: 7.5% at cost, trim above 15%, remove the initial-entry cap.

Diversification

Up to 20% in one sector. A target of 15–25 portfolio companies.

Thesis review

Each 20% decline from the average purchase price prompts a review by another analyst.

Liquidity Control

Position size no greater than five times daily trading volume. Preference for dual listings.

Cash Management

More cash when valuations are high. Disciplined deployment when opportunities emerge.

Investment decisions

Original: unanimous Committee approval. Proposal: majority approval; the chair retains a veto.

Investment universe

Investment universe: undervalued, small and growing

Three pillars with specific selection criteria.

01

Small/Mid Caps

  • Market capitalisation up to $25bn.
  • Revenue growth above 15% CAGR.
  • Flexibility in responding to change.
  • A strong competitive moat.

Portfolio examples: MongoDB (MDB), Descartes (DSGX).

02

Emerging Markets

  • CEE, Latin America and Asia.
  • Fast growth in population and consumption.
  • Less analyst coverage and potential mispricing.
  • Diversification beyond developed markets.

Portfolio examples: NLB, Kaspi.kz (KSPI), dLocal (DLO). We have also analysed Bilibili (BILI).

03

Equity Stories

  • Technology, geopolitical shifts and buyouts.
  • Opportunities overlooked or doubted by the market.
  • Asymmetric upside.
  • Strong catalysts for change.

Portfolio example: Advance Auto Parts (AAP) — a business turnaround.

Exclusion Criteria
Harmful businesses

Tobacco, weapons, gambling, and companies causing significant environmental or social harm

Structurally weak sectors

Chronically low ROIC industries like airlines with structural profitability challenges

Dishonest management

Any history of accounting fraud, integrity failures, or governance red flags
