Our process

Investment Process

From the first idea to a position we continue to follow.

1
Screening
Revenue growth, ROIC, margins and valuation. Then the business behind the numbers.
2
Analysis
A structured write-up, valuation and peer review.
3
Watchlist
The analyst team votes. The responsible analyst tracks fundamentals and valuation.
4
Investment
The Investment Committee decides on entry, followed by monitoring and monthly reporting.
Investment Approach

Our Strategy Pillars

Three complementary pillars. One portfolio of 15–25 companies.

No leverage

We invest without leverage to limit additional volatility and drawdowns.

No shorts

We focus on finding companies to own, without stock-borrow costs or margin calls.

No Hedging

We diversify across currencies and monitor FX exposure.

Active selection

We select companies individually across SMID caps, emerging markets and specific equity stories.

Target Allocation

SMID caps
Small and mid-cap companies
40%
Emerging Markets
CEE, Latin America and Asia
50%
Equity stories
Specific catalysts and special situations
10%

Long-term target weights at cost; cash allocation reflects available opportunities.

Risk framework

Risk Management

Position limits, diversification and independent thesis reviews.

September 2026 deck: original limits and proposed changes are shown separately; the proposal is not presented as adopted policy.

Position Sizing

Original: 5% at cost, trim above 10%; initial entry up to 2.5%. Proposal: 7.5% at cost, trim above 15%, remove the initial-entry cap.

Diversification

Up to 20% in one sector. A target of 15–25 portfolio companies.

Thesis review

Each 20% decline from the average purchase price prompts a review by another analyst.

Liquidity Control

Position size no greater than five times daily trading volume. Preference for dual listings.

Cash Management

More cash when valuations are high. Disciplined deployment when opportunities emerge.

Investment decisions

Original: unanimous Committee approval. Proposal: majority approval; the chair retains a veto.

Investment universe

Where We Invest

Less coverage. Local sources. Room for original research.

Small/Mid Caps

Market capitalisation below $25bn, strong revenue growth and a durable competitive advantage.

Emerging Markets

CEE, Latin America and Asia. We use local sources, language skills and regional contacts.

Equity Stories

Specific catalysts, changing market expectations and asymmetric potential.

Exclusion Criteria
Harmful businesses

Tobacco, weapons, gambling, and companies causing significant environmental or social harm

Structurally weak sectors

Chronically low ROIC industries like airlines with structural profitability challenges

Dishonest management

Any history of accounting fraud, integrity failures, or governance red flags
